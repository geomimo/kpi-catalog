# M2 spot check: first tagging run (15 Nov 2026)

> Kept as a record of the first run. The evaluation (M5) in `reports/ml_eval.md` replaces these numbers; `τ_min` is now 0.54.

First run of the ML tagger (`src/kpi_catalog/ml/tagger.py`, PRD §6) on the seed, for the mid-term status.
These are **preliminary numbers** from an untuned first run (`τ_min` = 0.45 picked by eye). The real
evaluation, with macro-F1, baselines and tuned thresholds, is task M5.

## What ran
- Model: `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2`. The input is `display_name` + `definition_text` + `formula`,
  compared by cosine similarity with each concept's prefLabel, altLabels and `skos:definition`.
- Scope: all **107** non-deprecated definitions. DEF-0025 (deprecated, M10) was skipped.
- Output: 128 theme tags (21 definitions also got a second theme) and 107 business-line tags in
  `definition_tags` (`source = ml`, `status = auto`), plus the definition embeddings in `instance/embeddings.npy`.

## T1 theme: good
- The top theme matches the gold label for **34 of 40** gold definitions (85%).
- The six misses:

  | Definition | Predicted (score) | Gold | Why |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | DEF-0202 Large Orders | Supply Chain (0.13) | Revenue | Very short dbt text ("Count of orders…"); lowest score in the run |
  | DEF-0203 Orders | Supply Chain (0.16) | Revenue | Same: the whole text is "Count of orders." |
  | DEF-0029 Inventory Amount | Supply Chain (0.59), Balance Sheet (0.48) | Balance Sheet | The gold theme came second |
  | DEF-0030 Investment Amount | Revenue (0.47) | Balance Sheet | "money invested" pulled it to Revenue |
  | DEF-0093 Resource Utilization Rate | Efficiency (0.72) | Workforce | High confidence, but disagrees with the gold label |
  | DEF-0094 Productivity Rate | Efficiency (0.68) | Workforce | Same |

- The definitions we wrote ourselves look right (they aren't scored, PRD §3.5): Turnover → Revenue,
  Active Users → Engagement, ATOPU → Revenue, Food Safety Incident Rate → Quality, Operating Expenses → Cost.
- The false-friend pair gets **different** themes, which is what M7 needs: *Inventory Amount* → Supply Chain,
  *Investment Amount* → Revenue.
- **Too many second themes.** 21 of 107 definitions got a second theme, compared with 5 of 40 in the gold set,
  and 8 of those 21 are Revenue. Every Inventory Turnover variant gets Revenue as its second theme, because
  Revenue has the altLabel *Turnover*. Raising `τ_min` (M5) should fix this.

## T2 business line: weak
- The predicted business line matches the gold label for **39 of 81** HF definitions (48%). Always guessing
  Group-wide also gets **38 of 81**, so T2 is no better than the majority-class baseline yet.
- The scores are low across the board (median about 0.26, against about 0.49 for themes). Most source texts
  are business-neutral ("total income generated by a product line"). In the HF data, the business line comes
  from the row's `domain`, not from anything the text says.
- The main confusions (gold → predicted): Stores → Group-wide (12), Group-wide → Banking (7),
  Group-wide → Membership (5), Own-brand Production → Group-wide (5). **Membership** is predicted 11 times
  but is the gold label only once: its concept text ("customers", "discounts", "subscription") attracts
  customer-related definitions.
